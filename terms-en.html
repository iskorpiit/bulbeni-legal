2. Your Account and Security

  • The information you provide in your profile (age, name, photos) must be real and your own.
  • Do not share your password with anyone; you are responsible for all activity on your account.
  • You can permanently delete your account at any time via Profile > "Delete My Account".

3. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on Bul Beni, and your account may be closed without warning if detected:

  • Nudity and sexual content: Sharing nude, semi-nude, or explicitly sexual photos/messages is prohibited.
  • Harassment and bullying: Threats, insults, degradation, and persistent unwanted contact are prohibited.
  • Hate speech: Discriminatory or hateful content based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, etc. is prohibited.
  • Violence: Real violent imagery, incitement to violence, or threats are prohibited.
  • Fake profiles and fraud: Creating a profile with fake photos/information, romance scams asking for money/gifts, and identity theft are prohibited.
  • Commercial use and spam: Using the App for advertising, selling products/services, promoting escort/prostitution services, or directing to another platform is prohibited.
  • Exploitation of minors: Any content or conduct involving persons under 18 is strictly prohibited and will be reported to authorities.
  • Sharing others' private information: Disclosing another user's phone number, address, or real identity without consent is prohibited.
  • Illegal activity: Organizing or promoting drug sales, weapons trafficking, or any other illegal activity is prohibited.

4. Reporting and Blocking

If you encounter a user who bothers you:

  • You can report it to us via the "Report" button on their profile,
  • You can "Block" them to completely stop them from contacting you.

We review reports and reserve the right to close accounts that violate our rules without prior notice.

⚠️ Emergency notice: If you feel you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency services first. Bul Beni is not an emergency service.

5. In-Person Meeting Safety

  • Always meet for the first time in a public, crowded place,
  • Tell a trusted friend or family member the meeting location/time in advance,
  • Arrange your own transportation,
  • Avoid sharing personal information (home address, workplace, financial situation) with someone you just met,
  • Trust your instincts — you always have the right to end a meeting if you feel uncomfortable.

Bul Beni cannot fully verify users' identities and is not responsible for interactions or meetings between people who met through the App. It is your responsibility to exercise caution.
