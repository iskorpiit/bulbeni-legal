Bul Beni — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Bul Beni (developer: Lipsoz) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards we apply to prevent, detect and respond to such content and behaviour in the Bul Beni app.

1. Adults only

  • Bul Beni is intended only for users aged 18 and over.
  • Date of birth is collected and checked at registration; users under 18 cannot create an account.
  • Accounts we believe belong to a minor are removed.

2. Prohibited content and behaviour

The following is strictly prohibited and results in immediate, permanent account removal:

  • Creating, sharing, requesting or linking to any CSAM, including drawn, generated or edited imagery;
  • Sexualising minors in any form, including text, photos, stories or profile content;
  • Grooming, soliciting or attempting to contact minors for sexual purposes;
  • Sextortion, trafficking or any other form of child exploitation.

3. In-app reporting

Users can report any other user directly in the app using the Report option in the menu on any user's profile, and can block users at any time. Reports related to child safety are prioritised for review.

4. How we respond

  • Reported content is reviewed promptly; confirmed CSAE/CSAM content is removed and the account is permanently banned.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and competent regional and national law enforcement, as required by applicable law.
  • We preserve relevant information as required by law to support investigations.

5. Legal compliance

Bul Beni complies with applicable child safety laws in the countries where it is available and cooperates with law enforcement requests.

6. Child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAM prevention matters:
Email: iskorpiit@gmail.com

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Türkçe — Çocuk Güvenliği Standartları

Bul Beni (geliştirici: Lipsoz), çocukların cinsel istismarı ve sömürüsüne (CSAE) ve çocuk istismarı içeren materyallere (CSAM) karşı sıfır tolerans uygular.

  • Uygulama yalnızca 18 yaş ve üzeri kullanıcılar içindir; kayıtta doğum tarihi kontrol edilir.
  • Çocukları cinselleştiren her türlü içerik, CSAM paylaşımı/talebi, çocuklarla cinsel amaçlı iletişim kurma girişimi kesinlikle yasaktır ve hesabın kalıcı olarak kapatılmasıyla sonuçlanır.
  • Kullanıcılar herhangi bir kullanıcıyı, profil menüsündeki Şikayet Et seçeneğiyle bildirebilir ve kullanıcıları engelleyebilir.
  • Doğrulanan CSAM içerikleri kaldırılır ve yasaların gerektirdiği şekilde NCMEC ile yetkili bölgesel/ulusal makamlara bildirilir.
  • Çocuk güvenliği iletişim adresi: iskorpiit@gmail.com

Deutsch — Kinderschutzstandards

Bul Beni (Entwickler: Lipsoz) verfolgt eine Null-Toleranz-Politik gegenüber sexuellem Missbrauch und Ausbeutung von Kindern (CSAE) sowie Darstellungen von sexuellem Kindesmissbrauch (CSAM).

  • Die App ist ausschließlich für Nutzer ab 18 Jahren; das Geburtsdatum wird bei der Registrierung geprüft.
  • Jegliche Sexualisierung von Minderjährigen, das Teilen oder Anfordern von CSAM sowie Kontaktversuche zu Minderjährigen zu sexuellen Zwecken sind streng verboten und führen zur dauerhaften Sperrung des Kontos.
  • Nutzer können andere Nutzer direkt in der App über die Option Melden im Profilmenü melden und Nutzer blockieren.
  • Bestätigte CSAM-Inhalte werden entfernt und gemäß geltendem Recht an NCMEC sowie die zuständigen regionalen und nationalen Behörden gemeldet.
  • Kontakt für Kinderschutz: iskorpiit@gmail.com

Русский — Стандарты защиты детей

Bul Beni (разработчик: Lipsoz) придерживается политики нулевой терпимости к сексуальному насилию и эксплуатации детей (CSAE) и материалам с сексуальным насилием над детьми (CSAM).

  • Приложение предназначено только для пользователей старше 18 лет; дата рождения проверяется при регистрации.
  • Любая сексуализация несовершеннолетних, распространение или запрос CSAM, а также попытки контакта с несовершеннолетними в сексуальных целях строго запрещены и ведут к бессрочной блокировке аккаунта.
  • Пользователи могут пожаловаться на любого пользователя прямо в приложении с помощью функции Пожаловаться в меню профиля, а также заблокировать пользователя.
  • Подтверждённые материалы CSAM удаляются, а информация передаётся в NCMEC и компетентные региональные и национальные органы в соответствии с законом.
  • Контакт по вопросам защиты детей: iskorpiit@gmail.com
