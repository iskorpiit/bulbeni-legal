Bul Beni — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data is collected, how it is used, who it is shared with, and what your rights are when you use the Bul Beni mobile app ("the App", "we"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

  • Name/Company: Lipsoz
  • Contact email: iskorpiit@gmail.com
  • Address: Not specified

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide at registration

  • Email address and password
  • Nickname (username), full name, date of birth, gender, nationality, marital status
  • About text (optional) and your interests (optional)
  • If you sign in with Google: the name and email address of your Google account (to prefill your full name and recognise your account)
  • Phone number (optional): if you choose to verify your profile. Your number is verified with an SMS code and stored only in our authentication system (Firebase Authentication); it is not written to the profile database and is never shown to other users. Others only see the ✓ "verified" badge on your profile. A number can only be used for one account.
  • Invite system: your personal invite code; if you signed up with an invite code, the user who invited you (to grant the invite reward — never shown to other users), and the number of friends you successfully invited.
  • Follows: the users you follow and the users who follow you. Only you can see these lists; the person you follow gets a "started following you" notification.

Your date of birth is stored in a separate record that only you can see; other users only see your age (you can hide it).

2.2 Profile and content information

  • Profile photos, gallery photos and temporary (24-hour) images you share as a "story"
  • Chat messages, photos, files and in-chat games (e.g. Tic-Tac-Toe) you send/receive
  • Activities you create or join
  • Posts, replies and likes you share in Nearby (Çevrem)
  • Your friendships and the users you block
  • Your virtual gift history and points balance

2.3 Location information

The App reads your location from your device's GPS but does not store your exact location or show it to other users. Your location is saved on the server rounded to about 1 km (0.01 degrees); only this approximate location is used on the map and for distance calculations. When you turn off Settings > Privacy > "Show me on the map", your stored location is deleted, you are hidden from the map and you will not receive nearby activity notifications meanwhile.

  • Activities: Everyone sees only the approximate area and the day; the exact time, meeting place and description are visible only to participants you approve.
  • Nearby posts: Saved with an approximate location so they can be shown to users near you.

You can turn off location permission at any time in your device settings.

2.4 Automatically collected information

  • Online/offline status and last seen time
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • In-app purchase and ad viewing history
  • App usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): events such as which screens and features are used

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and verify your account and give you access
  • To show users, activities and Nearby posts near you that match your interests
  • To provide messaging, gifts, in-chat games, friendships and activities
  • To send you notifications
  • To translate messages and posts into your language: the text is sent through our servers to Google Cloud Translation (a daily limit per user applies)
  • To process in-app purchases and ad rewards
  • To review aggregated usage statistics to improve the App
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fake and multiple accounts, and to review reports

5. Data Retention

Your data is kept while your account is active. Nearby posts and stories disappear after 24 hours; Nearby posts and their replies are deleted from the server automatically.

You can delete your account at any time in the App via Profile > Settings > "Delete My Account" — this cannot be undone. Deletion runs on our servers and removes: your profile, photos and story, your date of birth, your activities and your participation in other activities, your friend requests, your Nearby posts, replies and likes, the chats you took part in (with their messages and attachments, for both sides), and finally your account itself (including your linked phone number and Google sign-in, if any).

After your account is deleted, we keep only:

  • Purchase records (which points package was bought and when): for financial record-keeping and to prevent the same purchase from being reused.
  • An irreversible hash (SHA-256) of your email address, not the address itself: to prevent the "first activity join is free" benefit and the invite reward from being claimed again by deleting and re-creating an account.
  • Reports you made and reports about you: for user safety and possible legal requests.

6. Your Rights

  • You can view and update most of your profile info (full name and "about" are fixed after registration; nickname and date of birth cannot be changed once added). You can see your account details in Profile > Settings,
  • You can control the visibility of your photos, of your age/gender/marital status, and whether you appear on the map,
  • You can block and report users, remove friends, and delete chats/messages,
  • You can permanently delete your account directly in the App via Profile > Settings > "Delete My Account".

Depending on where you live (KVKK, GDPR, etc.), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, object to processing of, and port your data. To exercise these rights or for questions: iskorpiit@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

Bul Beni is intended only for users aged 18 and over. Date-of-birth verification is performed at registration.

8. Data Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard security measures (encrypted transmission, access control).

9. International Data Transfer

Your data may be processed and stored on Google's global server infrastructure (Firebase), which may include servers outside your country.

10. Advertising

The App displays rewarded ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect information such as an advertising ID for ad personalization. You can limit ad personalization from your device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you within the app of any material changes.

12. Contact Us

For questions: iskorpiit@gmail.com
