Bul Beni — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data is collected, how it is used, who it is shared with, and what your rights are when you use the Bul Beni mobile app ("the App", "we"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

  • Name/Company: Lipsoz
  • Contact email: iskorpiit@gmail.com
  • Address: Not specified

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide at registration

  • Email address and password
  • Name, date of birth, gender, nationality, marital status
  • Bio text (optional) and your interests (optional)

2.2 Profile and content information

  • Profile photos, gallery photos, and temporary (24-hour) "story" images you share
  • Chat messages, photos, and files you send/receive
  • Activities you create or join
  • Your friendship relationships
  • Your virtual gift sending/receiving history and points balance

2.3 Location information

The App collects your GPS location to show nearby users on the map. This location is shown to other users according to the distance/visibility setting you choose, and is used to send nearby-activity notifications matching your interests. You can turn off location permission from your device settings at any time.

2.4 Automatically collected information

  • Online/offline status and last-seen time
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • In-app purchase and rewarded-ad viewing history

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, verify, and give you access to your account
  • To show you nearby users/activities matching your interests
  • To provide messaging, gifting, friendship, and activity features
  • To send you notifications
  • To translate messages in different languages (Google Cloud Translation API)
  • To process in-app purchases and ad rewards
  • To detect and prevent abuse and fake accounts

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your data. The following service providers process data to make the App work:

Additionally, other users can see your name, profile info (subject to your privacy settings), photos, location, and the messages you send/receive with them.

5. Data Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account instantly, at any time, from within the app via Profile > "Delete My Account" — this action is irreversible. Some records may be kept longer where required by law.

6. Your Rights

  • You can view and update most of your profile information (name and "bio" are fixed after registration),
  • You can control the visibility of your photos and your age/gender/marital status information,
  • You can block users, unfriend, and delete chats/messages,
  • You can permanently delete your account instantly from within the app via Profile > "Delete My Account".

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, and object to the processing of your data. For questions, contact: iskorpiit@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

Bul Beni is intended only for users aged 18 and over. Date-of-birth verification is performed at registration.

8. Data Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard security measures (encrypted transmission, access control).

9. International Data Transfer

Your data may be processed and stored on Google's global server infrastructure (Firebase), which may include servers outside your country.

10. Advertising

The App displays rewarded ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect information such as an advertising ID for ad personalization. You can limit ad personalization from your device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you within the app of any material changes.

12. Contact Us

For questions: iskorpiit@gmail.com
