Bul Beni — Privacy Notice on the Processing of Personal Data (KVKK)

Effective: November 2026 · Version 2026-11

This notice is provided under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") to inform you how your personal data is processed when you use the Bul Beni mobile app. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.

1. Data Controller

  • Data controller: Lipsoz
  • Address: Garipçe Mahallesi, Fehim Sk. No:69/1, Sarıyer, İstanbul, Türkiye
  • Email: iskorpiit@gmail.com

2. Personal Data Processed

  • Identity: full name, nickname, age and date of birth (your date of birth is visible only to you).
  • Contact: email address; optionally a verified phone number (never shown to other users).
  • Profile: gender, nationality, marital status, "about" text, interests, photos and stories.
  • Location: approximate location (rounded to about 1 km); you can hide yourself on the map.
  • User content: messages and attachments, posts and replies, activities, gifts, friendships and follows.
  • Security: session data, notification token, an irreversible hash of your email address, block and report records.
  • Transactions: in-app point purchases and point history.
  • Only with your explicit consent: usage analytics data and the advertising ID for personalized ads (see the Explicit Consent Text).

3. Purposes

  • Creating and managing your account,
  • Providing the map, activities, matching, messaging, posts and other app services,
  • Handling points, purchases, invitations and badges,
  • Keeping the service secure, preventing fake accounts, fraud and abuse, moderating content,
  • Handling requests and complaints, meeting legal obligations,
  • Only if you consent: analysing how the app is used and showing you personalized ads.

4. Legal Grounds

Your data is processed under Article 5(2) KVKK: (c) it is directly necessary to conclude or perform a contract (your membership and the service), (ç) compliance with a legal obligation, (e) establishing, exercising or protecting a right, (f) legitimate interest (security and abuse prevention), provided it does not harm your fundamental rights. Usage analytics and personalized ads rely solely on your explicit consent under Article 5(1).

5. How Data Is Collected

Electronically and partly automatically, through the forms you fill in, the device permissions you grant (location, camera, notifications) and your use of the app.

6. Transfers

  • Service providers: the app runs on Google LLC services (Firebase, Google Maps, Cloud Translation, AdMob, Google Play billing). Their servers may be located abroad; your data is transferred abroad for these purposes in line with Article 9 KVKK.
  • Other users: what you share on your profile is visible to other users according to your settings (e.g. approximate location, profile photo, badges).
  • Authorities: competent public authorities where legally required.

7. Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists; chats with no message for 24 months and accounts not signed in to for 24 months (after 30 days' notice) are deleted automatically. When you delete your account in the app, your data is deleted; exceptions such as purchase records, email hashes and reports and their retention periods are described in the Privacy Policy.

8. Your Rights (Article 11 KVKK)

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
  • Request deletion or destruction under the conditions of Article 7 KVKK,
  • Request that corrections and deletions be notified to third parties it was transferred to,
  • Object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis,
  • Claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

9. How to Apply

Send requests about your rights from the email address registered in the app to iskorpiit@gmail.com, or in writing to the address above. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
