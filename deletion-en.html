Bul Beni — Account Deletion

If you want to permanently delete your Bul Beni account and all data associated with it, there are two ways to do so:

Method 1 — From within the app (recommended, instant):
Open the Bul Beni app → go to the Profile tab → scroll down and tap "Delete My Account" → follow the on-screen confirmation steps (text confirmation + password). Your account and all data are deleted instantly and permanently.
Method 2 — If you don't have access to the app (by email):
Send an email to iskorpiit@gmail.com from your registered email address, with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Your request will be processed manually within 30 days at the latest.

What data is deleted?

  • Your profile information (name, age, gender, nationality, marital status, bio, interests)
  • All photos and stories you uploaded
  • Messages you sent/received and your chat history
  • Your friendships
  • Your gift sending/receiving history and points balance
  • Activities you created
  • Your location data

What data may be retained?

Due to legal obligations (e.g. fraud/abuse report records), some limited data may be kept for the legally required retention period. All other personal data is completely deleted.

Is uninstalling the app enough?

No — removing the app from your phone (uninstall) does NOT delete your account or your data on our servers. To fully delete your account and data, you need to use one of the two methods above (we recommend using "Delete My Account" in the app).

For questions: iskorpiit@gmail.com
